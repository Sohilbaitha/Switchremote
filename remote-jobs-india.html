Good to know

Is Switchremote free?

Yes. Job seekers never pay anything, and you don't need an account. Employers can post remote jobs for free too.

I'm a fresher with no experience. Can I apply?

That's who this site is for. We leave out senior roles and jobs that ask for two or more years of experience. Do read the full listing before you apply, because the employer decides the final requirements.

Are these jobs really remote?

We only list jobs that the employer or job board has marked as remote. Some are open to people in any country and some only to certain countries, so every job shows where you can apply from.

How do you keep scam jobs out?

Jobs come only from company career pages, employers we review by hand, and established remote job boards. From job boards we show a job only when we have checked the company first. Our system also removes listings that ask for money or push you to WhatsApp or Telegram. No filter is perfect, so if something looks off, tap Report on the job or use the scam checker.

How often is the list updated?

Every 6 hours. Jobs that have closed or become too old are removed at the same time.
