Privacy Policy

This page describes privacy considerations for visitors to Switchremote, an independent job-discovery website.

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Browsing the website

You can browse job listings without creating an account or submitting information through a contact form on this website.

Messages and social platforms

If you contact Switchremote through Telegram, LinkedIn, Instagram, or X, the platform you use processes your message under its own privacy policy and settings. Please share only the information needed for your enquiry.

Analytics and cookies

Google Analytics has not been configured for this website as of the date above. If analytics or other tracking tools are added later, this policy should be updated to explain the tools in use. Website hosting and browser services may process technical request data as part of delivering pages.

Job listings and external websites

Application links may lead to employer websites, job boards, or other third-party services. Those websites have their own privacy policies and terms. Check the destination URL before submitting an application or personal information.

Data security and retention

Switchremote does not ask visitors to create an account to browse listings. Messages sent through third-party platforms are subject to those platforms' data handling and retention practices. No online service can guarantee complete security.

Policy updates and questions

This policy may change if the website's features or data practices change. For privacy questions, use the Contact page.
