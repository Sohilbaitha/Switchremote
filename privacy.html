Privacy Policy

This page explains, in plain language, what to keep in mind when visiting Switchremote. We aim to make job discovery easier while being transparent about external services and links.

Last updated: 22 October 2026

1. About Switchremote

Switchremote is an independent job-discovery website. We publish job listing information so visitors can discover opportunities and, where available, follow links to the employer or original listing source. We are not the employer for the vacancies shown on this website.

2. Information you provide

You can browse the site without submitting a contact form on this website. If you contact us through a third-party platform such as Telegram, LinkedIn, Instagram, or X, that platform processes your message under its own privacy policy and settings. Please do not send passwords, financial details, identity documents, or other sensitive personal information through social media.

3. Analytics and cookies

As of the date above, Google Analytics has not been configured for this website. This policy should be updated if analytics, advertising, cookie-based measurement, or other tracking tools are added in the future. Your browser, hosting provider, or third-party services may process technical request data as part of delivering a website; the exact data handled depends on those services and their settings.

4. Job listings and external links

Job details and application links may lead to employer websites, job boards, or other third-party services. Those sites have their own privacy policies and terms, which we do not control. Check the destination URL and the employer’s official careers page before sharing information or applying. Switchremote cannot guarantee the accuracy, availability, legitimacy, or continued existence of every external listing.

5. Data retention and security

We do not ask visitors to create an account to browse job listings. Messages you send through external social platforms may remain subject to those platforms’ retention and deletion practices. No website or online communication method can be guaranteed completely secure, so share only the information needed to explain your question or report.

6. Children

This website is intended for people exploring employment opportunities. It is not designed to knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

7. Changes to this policy

We may revise this page if website features or data practices change. The “Last updated” date at the top indicates the latest stated revision date.

8. Privacy questions

For privacy questions or corrections, contact us through our Contact page. You can also learn more about the site on the About page.
