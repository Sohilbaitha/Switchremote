Reporting a job listing

When contacting us about a listing, share its title, the company name, the page URL, and what appears incorrect (for example, a broken application link, expired vacancy, or misleading details). Never send passwords, identity documents, bank details, or other sensitive personal information through social media.

For employers

If you represent an employer and want to suggest a listing or request a correction, contact us through LinkedIn or Telegram. Please use an official company account or public careers-page link where possible. A message does not guarantee that a job will be published.

Other information

Switchremote is an independent job-discovery website. We are not the employer for the vacancies listed, and we cannot control an employer’s hiring decisions or application process.
