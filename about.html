About Switchremote

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Switchremote is where remote jobs meet remote talent. It is a free job board for people who want to work from home. We focus on remote jobs that freshers can apply for. You pick where you live and we show the jobs open to you. Right now you can pick India, the Philippines, Indonesia, Vietnam, Malaysia or Thailand, or see jobs that are open to people in any country.

We collect jobs from public job feeds, company career pages and employers who post with us. The list refreshes automatically every 6 hours. When you tap Apply, you go straight to the original listing, on the employer's page or on the job board that published it.

Switchremote is run by Sohil Baitha in Delhi, India.

How we keep jobs safe

Your safety matters more to us than the number of jobs on this page. Here is what we do:

  • Trusted sources only. Jobs come from job boards that check employers (Remotive, Jobicy, Himalayas and Remote OK) or straight from a company's own careers page.
  • You apply on the real page. When you tap Apply, you go to the employer's or job board's own page. We never ask for your CV, documents or details.
  • Automatic safety checks. Every few hours our system removes jobs that have closed, and jobs that ask for money, pay nothing or only commission, send you to WhatsApp or Telegram, use shortened or suspicious links, or have almost no details.
  • Only companies we have checked. A job from a job board is shown only when the company is a well-known employer on our approved list. Companies we do not recognise stay hidden until we have checked them. We leave out staffing and outsourcing agencies, crypto companies, and data labelling or AI annotation work, and any company that breaks our rules is blocked for good.
  • Fresher jobs only. Our system reads the whole listing, not just the title. A job is shown only when it gives a clear sign that it is a starting role, such as intern, trainee, junior, entry-level or "freshers can apply". Jobs that require previous experience are left out. A job that asks for 1 or 2 years stays only when the listing itself says freshers can apply.
  • No hidden employers. We leave out listings posted by an agency for an unnamed "client", so you always know which company you are applying to.
  • Employer posts are reviewed by hand. Every job sent through our form is checked by us before it goes live.
  • You can report anything. Every job has a Report button. We look at every report and remove anything that is wrong.

No system is perfect, so always check the company yourself before sharing personal details.

Where our jobs come from, and credits

We only use sources that allow their jobs to be listed, and we credit every one of them:

  • Company career pages. Public job boards that companies publish themselves through Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workable, Recruitee and SmartRecruiters. Apply opens the company's own page.
  • Remote job boards. Remotive, Jobicy, Himalayas and Remote OK, through the public feeds they offer for this purpose. Each of these jobs is marked "via" the board it came from, and Apply opens the job on that board.
  • Employers. Companies that post a job with us through our form.

For each job we show only the title, company, country, a few basic facts and a short extract. The full listing stays on the source's own page. We do not copy jobs from sites that do not allow it, and we do not pass these jobs on to other job sites.

If you run one of these sources or companies and want a listing changed or removed, contact us and we will do it.
