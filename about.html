About Switchremote

Switchremote is an independent job-discovery website designed to help people find remote opportunities and check where a role may be available.

What we do

We collect selected remote job listings from public job APIs and employer career pages, then display available details such as the role title, company, source, location and application link. The job feed is updated automatically when the scheduled job workflow runs successfully.

Who can use it?

Listings may be relevant to applicants in India, Indonesia, the Philippines, Vietnam, Malaysia and Thailand, as well as roles described by their source as worldwide. Eligibility varies by role. A country filter or general “worldwide” label is not a guarantee that an employer will accept every applicant from that country.

Check before applying

Job information can change or expire after it is collected. Always open the original employer or job-source listing to confirm that the vacancy is still open, the location is eligible, and the role details are accurate. Switchremote is not an employer, recruiter or hiring representative and cannot guarantee an interview or job offer.

Stay safe: Never pay a fee to secure a job, and never share an OTP, password or banking credentials with someone claiming to recruit you.

Independence and transparency

Switchremote is an independent discovery service. Company names, logos and trademarks belong to their respective owners. Their appearance on this website does not necessarily mean they endorse or partner with Switchremote.

Corrections and questions

If a listing looks incorrect or expired, please use one of the social links on the homepage to reach Switchremote. A dedicated email contact may be added later.
